Hello! I am Emanuele Petriglia, a second-year PhD student at the [University of Milano-Bicocca](https://en.unimib.it/emanuele-petriglia). My research interests center on **Reinforcement Learning** and **Cloud Computing**. I am part of the [NetLab](https://netlab.disco.unimib.it/) (formerly [REDS laboratory](https://redslab.altervista.org/)), working under the supervision of Prof. [Marco Savi](https://marcosavi.altervista.org/) and Prof. [Michele Ciavotta](https://en.unimib.it/michele-ciavotta).

Currently, I am investigating the use of Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for workload distribution in Function-as-a-Service platforms, particularly DFaaS, which I also help maintain.

In my free time, I enjoy cycling, I am a longtime Linux user (here you can find my [dotfiles](https://gitlab.com/ema-pe/dotfiles)), and I like contributing to open-source projects.
